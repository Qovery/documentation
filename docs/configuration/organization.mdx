An organization is a shared account where developers can collaborate across many projects. Organization owners and admins can manage all aspects of the organization, from clusters to member access.

## Prerequisites

To follow this section, you must have [created an account](https://console.qovery.com/signup).

## Creating an Organization

### When Signing Up

When you sign up for Qovery, you will be asked to create your first organization and project. You will need to:

1. Sign in through your Git provider (GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket), Google or Microsoft
2. Fill in your profile: full name, a work email (personal email domains are rejected), and company name. Submitting creates your organization and a first project automatically
3. Choose how to set up your first cluster: connect your own cloud account if you have cloud admin access, or set up a local demo cluster otherwise

You start on the Scale plan during your trial. For more information on available plans, visit the [pricing page](https://www.qovery.com/pricing).

Qovery also has a Free plan, with no time limit and no credit card: 3 users plus unlimited AI agents, 1 cluster of your own, and 1,000 deployment minutes a month with a hard stop: at the limit, new deployments pause until next month or until you upgrade, and Free never bills overage. It includes preview environments and environment cloning, with community support. See the [pricing page](https://www.qovery.com/pricing) for the current plans and limits.

## Change an Organization

If you have access to multiple organizations, you can switch between them:

1. Locate the organization dropdown in the header
2. Click on the dropdown to see all available organizations
3. Select the organization you want to switch to

## Delete an Organization

<Warning>
  Deleting an organization is **irreversible**. All data associated with the
  organization will be permanently deleted.
</Warning>

To delete an organization:

1. Navigate to Organization Settings
2. Scroll to the "Danger Zone" section
3. Click "Delete Organization"
4. Confirm the deletion by typing the organization name

## Billing

Manage your organization's billing and subscription:

- **View Invoices**: Access all past and current invoices
- **Payment Methods**: Add or update credit cards and payment details
- **Subscription**: Upgrade or downgrade your plan
- **Usage**: Monitor resource usage and costs

During the trial, add a credit card to activate your organization on one of the **Starter**, **Growth**, or **Scale** plans, billed monthly once the trial ends. You can switch between these plans until the trial ends.

<Info>
When you create an organization or add a payment card, you must provide your billing details, including a billing address. A **VAT number** is required for billing addresses in EU countries and optional elsewhere. Postal codes are validated against the expected format for the selected country.
</Info>

For pricing details, visit the [pricing page](https://www.qovery.com/pricing).

## Agents

The **Agents** tab gathers in one place how your team's coding agents work with Qovery. Open it from your organization's tabs, next to **Audit Logs**: it opens on the **Skills registry**. Everything about agents lives here: the old Organization settings pages for the Copilot, skills, skill presets, agent activity, policy tokens and the Agent group redirect to it.

The idea behind it: agents can do real infrastructure work on their own when every operation is checked at the API. Agents sets that up in three parts: **Guide** agents with the Qovery skills, tailored per team and per person with skill presets; **Control** them with policy tokens, so each agent gets exactly the permissions its job needs, checked on every API call and denied by default; and **Observe** what they did and what was blocked, in Activity and the audit logs.

Its side menu has the Skills registry, Automations and Activity, then a **Settings** group:

- **Skills registry**: where your organization's [skill presets](/copilot/skill-presets) (Preview) live, its own version of the Qovery skills. One page holds the list of presets, which you can sort and filter, the editor, a preview side panel that shows what a member or a role receives, and each preset's history. New presets start from scratch or from a template per user profile. Members see a read-only **What your agent receives** view instead. Skill loads are in the [audit logs](/getting-started/security-and-compliance/audit-logs), and usage is in **Activity**.
- **Automations** (where [Agent Tasks](/configuration/agent-tasks/overview) are available): the organization's agent tasks, with their project and environment. Owners and Admins can **Create agent task**, which asks for the environment it runs in. To run one of the [agent policy examples](/configuration/organization/policy-examples/overview) on its own, see [Run it on its own](/configuration/organization/policy-examples/overview#run-it-on-its-own).
- **Activity**: one view of what members' agents did (through the skills and the MCP server) and what policy tokens did, allowed and denied. Its menu entry shows a badge with the number of denied calls in the last 7 days. One toolbar filters by period, actor (everyone, all members, all policy tokens, or one member or token), skill, source and outcome. The filters are kept in the URL, so you can share a filtered view. **Recent actions** sits right under the chart, in pages, with an **Action type** filter. Break the activity down by member, by token or by skill, and click a row to filter the whole page. **What was blocked** lists the refused calls, with **Show all denied**. See [Activity](/copilot/skill-presets#activity).
- **Settings**, shown to the roles that can use at least one of its pages:
  - **Policy tokens** (Owners, Admins and DevOps): the organization's [API Policy Tokens](/configuration/organization/api-policy-token), managed entirely here. **Create policy token** opens the form. Each token shows its role, a bar of its last 7 days (allowed and denied calls per day), when it was last used and when it expires, with a **No expiry** warning badge for a token that never expires. Its row menu has **See activity** (Activity filtered on that token), **View policy** (read-only: a policy cannot change after creation) and **Delete token** (you confirm by typing the token's name). Deleted tokens that made calls in the last 30 days are listed greyed out.
  - **Copilot settings** (Beta, Owners and Admins): what the [Console AI Copilot](/copilot/console) does, its status, and its settings: access source, access mode, scheduled tasks, and **Turn off Copilot**.
  - **Model tokens** and **MCP servers**, where [Agent Tasks](/configuration/agent-tasks/overview) are available, shared with every agent task in the organization. See [Agent Tasks: model tokens and MCP servers](#agent-tasks-model-tokens-and-mcp-servers).

**Connect an agent**, in the Skills registry header, opens a window with three tabs: **Qovery skills** (the install command), **CLI** (install and sign in), and **MCP server**, with the setup for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI and VS Code.

**Who sees what**

| Page | Owners and Admins | Other members |
|------|-------------------|---------------|
| Skills registry | Create, edit, assign, preview and see the history of presets | **What your agent receives**, read-only |
| Automations | Yes, with **Create agent task** | The list, without **Create agent task** |
| Activity | The whole organization, members and policy tokens | **Your activity**: their own, with no actor filter and no token breakdown |
| Settings | Policy tokens, Copilot settings, model tokens and MCP servers | DevOps: Policy tokens. Model tokens and MCP servers where Agent Tasks are available |

Old links keep working: the former Agents overview and the usage, skill loads and assignments pages redirect to the Skills registry or to Activity, and the former connect page opens the Skills registry with the **Connect an agent** window open.

### Turn on AI

AI features are the organization's opt-in to AI. The Copilot needs them, and so does the security review that checks every skill preset before it is saved. New organizations have AI features on by default.

When AI features are off, Owners and Admins see **Turn on AI** in the Skills registry and on **Copilot settings**. They turn everything on in one step:

1. Click **Turn on AI**.
2. Read the summary of the Copilot terms, open the full terms if you want, accept them and confirm.

This one confirmation turns on the organization's AI features and the Copilot, in read-only mode. To allow actions such as deploys and restarts, change the access mode later in **Settings → Copilot settings**. If the Copilot cannot be turned on, AI features stay on and you can retry from the same page.

Other members cannot turn on AI.

### Agent Tasks: model tokens and MCP servers

<AgentTasksEvolutionNote />

Under **Agents → Settings**, Owners and Admins manage what's shared with every [Agent Task](/configuration/agent-tasks/overview) in the organization: AI provider tokens under **Model tokens**, and MCP servers under **MCP servers**.

**Model tokens**: create and manage the AI provider tokens used by agent tasks. Qovery currently supports [Claude](https://www.anthropic.com/) (Anthropic), [AWS Bedrock](https://aws.amazon.com/bedrock/), and Anthropic-compatible gateways as providers. See [Choose a Provider and Model](/configuration/agent-tasks/overview#creating-an-agent) for the Anthropic-compatible Base URL and limits. A token created here, or created on the fly from an agent task's **Choose a Provider** step, is saved to this list and can be reused by any other agent task in the organization instead of re-entering credentials each time.

**MCP servers**: connect any remote HTTPS MCP server here (name, URL, and headers) to make it available when configuring an individual agent.

<Info>
Qovery's own MCP server is added here automatically, organization-scoped and read-only, as soon as any agent task adds a Qovery service as Context, see [Add MCP Servers](/configuration/agent-tasks/overview#creating-an-agent). To add it yourself instead, for example with write access, use `https://mcp.qovery.com/mcp` and authenticate with an [API Policy Token](/configuration/organization/api-policy-token) (a regular API token works too).
</Info>

### General Information

Configure basic organization details:

- **Company name**: Your organization's official name
- **Description**: Brief description of your organization
- **Website**: Your company website URL
- **Admin contact emails**: Enter one or multiple email addresses separated by commas

<Info>
  Admin contact emails are used for communications about infrastructure outages,
  maintenance updates, and weekly and monthly usage reports.
</Info>

Click **Update** to save your changes.

### Moved to Agents

The Copilot, skills, skill presets, agent activity (now **Activity**), policy tokens and the **Agent** group (model tokens and MCP servers for Agent Tasks) are no longer in Organization settings. They are in the [Agents](#agents) tab, and the old settings links redirect there. Regular [API tokens](/configuration/organization/api-token) stay in Organization settings, under **API token**.
